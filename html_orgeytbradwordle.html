How To Play

Guess the word or phrase in 6 tries. The color of the tiles will change to show how close your guess was.

C

Cyan means the letter is in the word and in the correct spot.

O

Orange means the letter is in the word but in the wrong spot.

D

Dark Gray means the letter is not in the word in any spot.

_

Darker blocks are spaces for phrases. They skip automatically.
