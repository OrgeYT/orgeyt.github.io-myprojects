About DFJK Retake

This tool is designed to help you practice note patterns and key rhythms (inspired by games like FNF — Friday Night Funkin' — and Osu!Mania). It's intended for practicing NOTE patterns, not as a substitute for full gameplay skill, timing judgement, or in-depth mechanics.

  • Play as fast as you want — customize the chart length and bot NPS.
  • Edit keybinds, note skins and color palettes to match your preferred layout.
  • Enable doubles, multis or jacks to practice chords and repeated lanes.
  • Use the Bot Mode to auto-play or to generate practice patterns.
  • The side panel shows per-key KPS (keys per second) and visual feedback.

Tip: Use the SAVE and LOAD buttons to export/import your settings as YAML files for consistent practice sessions.
