Lyric bench

Click to place a note, drag to move, drag the right edge to resize, right-click or double-click to delete. Space moves to the next note (and adds one at the end). End a syllable with “-” to keep it joined to the next in subtitles. Write phones directly with braces, like {S T AE1 N D}.

Type anything and the same built-in voice engine reads it in a speaking voice. Capitals inside a word are spelled out as letters, so OrgeYT is read “Orge why tee”.
