Breath if Prime — Original concept by Carykh.

Every second the number increases by 1. All living players lose 1 air. If the number is prime, every living player gains their “Air/Prime” amount (cannot exceed capacity). Die at 0 air → grave appears permanently.

This is a fan simulation. Concept by Carykh.
